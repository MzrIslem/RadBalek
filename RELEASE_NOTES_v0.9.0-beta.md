# Rad Balek v0.9.0-beta — رد بالك

**Final beta** of Rad Balek — early warning for Algeria: heatwaves, wildfires,
floods, earthquakes, storms and road danger, across all 58 wilayas.

> ⚠️ **Not an official government service.** Rad Balek is an independent tool that
> relays publicly available alerts. It does **not** replace official instructions.
> In an emergency, call **Protection Civile: 14 or 1021**.

## What's inside

- 🔴 **Red-alert siren that rings through silent mode.** A red alert takes over the
  lockscreen with a full-screen alert and a civil-defense siren on the alarm
  stream — it wakes you even if the phone is muted.
- 🗣️ **Spoken announcements** in Arabic and French, for anyone who can't read the
  screen.
- 🗺️ **Live hazard map** — official ONM vigilance, satellite wildfire detection
  (NASA FIRMS) with fire intensity, earthquakes (EMSC + CRAAG), and field reports.
- 📡 **Algerian sources** — Protection Civile (dgpc.dz), plus Algerian press for
  floods, road closures and collapses the weather feeds miss (clearly marked
  *non-official*).
- 📞 **One-tap emergency** — 14 / 17 / 1055, "I'm safe" SMS to family, and direct
  calling from your contacts.

## Install

1. Download **`app-release.apk`** below.
2. On your phone, allow installing from your browser/files (Android will prompt).
3. Open the app, then go to **Réglages → Mode urgence** and enable **both**:
   - Autoriser en mode Ne pas déranger
   - Ignorer l'optimisation batterie
   
   *(On OnePlus / Xiaomi / Huawei the battery exemption is what lets a red alert
   reach you at night.)*
4. Test it: **Réglages → Tester mes notifications**, then lock the screen — the
   siren should sound after ~8 seconds, even in silent mode.

Android 8+ · ~54 MB · works on all devices.

## Privacy

Built privacy-by-design, in line with **Loi n° 18-07** (Algeria's data-protection
law) and GDPR principles: minimal data, no account, no tracking, no third-party
sharing. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
